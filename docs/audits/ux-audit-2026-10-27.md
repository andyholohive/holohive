# HoloHive Portal — UI/UX audit, 2026-10-27

**Method.** Source-level audit of all 59 pages under `app/` plus `components/`, cross-checked against the sidebar, the convention linter, and live row counts in production. The browser pane was not signed in, so nothing here is from clicking through the live app. Items marked *verify* need a look at the real screen.

**Headline.** The visual language is consistent and the convention linter is clean (0 violations across 826 files). The weaknesses are structural: dead routes and duplicated navigation, missing empty states on the pages people land on first, legibility (1,200+ uses of 9–10px text), weak mobile handling outside the sidebar, and forms that lose work silently. Two workflow problems found this week (KR Signal approvals ignored for 8 weeks, ops zeroing auto-created payments by hand) are UX problems too, and bigger than any pixel issue.

---

## Ranked findings

### 1. Navigation carries dead weight — 21 routes unreachable, 12 with no inbound link at all
40 sidebar items across 8 sections, and still 21 built routes are in neither the sidebar nor the customize registry. Twelve have zero inbound links anywhere in the app, so nobody can reach them except by typing the URL:

| Route | Last touched | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `/crm/meetings`, `/channel-partners`, `/ai-insights` | 06-29 | Delete or park |
| `/tasks/my-dashboard`, `/tasks/templates` | 06-05 | Delete (superseded by HQ + Templates) |
| `/tasks/admin` | 06-15 | Delete or fold into `/admin` |
| `/clients/templates` | 08-05 | Delete or link from Templates |
| `/intelligence/coverage` | 07-27 | Link from Intelligence or delete |
| `/admin/content-tags`, `/admin/short-links`, `/admin/telegram-comm`, `/admin/field-options` | — | `/admin` hub has no links to them. Add cards. |
| `/test-ai` | 05-29 | Delete from prod |

Also: **Pipeline (Board) and Legacy Sales sit side by side** in the Sales section. Two boards for one concept is the kind of thing a new teammate gets wrong on day one. Legacy Sales was last touched 07-26 and the new Pipeline shipped its v1.3 this week. Decide a retirement date and hide it from non-admins now.

**Fix:** ~2 hours. Delete the dead routes, add the four admin cards, hide Legacy Sales behind super_admin.

### 2. First-screen pages have no empty state
22 pages render nothing (or a bare zero) when data is missing, including the ones a new user lands on: `/dashboard`, `/analytics`, `/initiatives`, `/crm/pipeline` (has ad-hoc text), `/channel-partners`, `/settings`. A new client with no campaign, or a quiet week, shows blank cards with no explanation and no next action. The shared `EmptyState` exists and is used on 37 pages, so this is adoption, not design.

**Fix:** ~3 hours for the eight pages that matter (`/dashboard` four tabs, `/analytics`, `/initiatives`, `/crm/pipeline`, `/intelligence`).

### 3. Legibility: 1,212 uses of 9–10px text
Top files: `/kols` 54, `DiscoveryPanel` 52, `/mindshare` 43, `/forms` 40, `KoreaSignalsPanel` 34, `/lists` 34, `ContentDashboardTableView` 31. 10px is below every readability guideline, and Korean glyphs at 10px lose their strokes on non-Retina displays. The v11 spec uses `text-[10px] uppercase tracking-[0.18em]` for column kickers, which is fine for a 5-letter header, but it has spread to values, chips, metadata lines and whole table bodies.

**Fix:** Rule: 10px only for uppercase tracked kickers; everything carrying a value or a name is 12px minimum. One pass per top file, ~1 day total. *Verify* on a 1080p external monitor, which is what the team uses.

### 4. Mobile is only handled at the shell
The sidebar has a proper drawer and topbar. Below that: 1 page in the whole app has a table-to-card fallback, 20 table files have no horizontal scroll wrapper (the row just overflows the card), and 23 fixed `grid-cols-3/4/5` lines in 15 files never collapse. Internal admin pages on a phone are a secondary concern. **The public pages are not**: clients open `/public/portal/[id]`, `/public/campaigns/[id]` and the renewal packs from Telegram on a phone. Those files are 4,042 and 3,273 lines and carry 53 and 48 hardcoded hex colors each, which means they've drifted from the token system that makes responsive fixes cheap.

**Fix:** Phase 1 (half day): wrap the 20 tables in `overflow-x-auto`, add `md:` prefixes to the 23 grids. Phase 2 (2 days): a real phone pass on the three public surfaces, with screenshots. *Verify* on a real device, not the devtools emulator.

### 5. Forms lose work silently
114 dialogs, 16 slide-overs, zero `beforeunload` or dirty-state guards anywhere. Radix dialogs close on Escape and on outside click by default. The heavy forms (New Client, KOL profile, form builder at 3,505 lines, campaign settings, meeting notes with 14 state fields) throw away everything typed on a mis-click. This is the single most likely source of "the portal ate my input" complaints.

**Fix:** one `useUnsavedGuard(isDirty)` hook that blocks outside-click close and asks before Escape/navigation; wire it into the six heaviest dialogs. ~4 hours.

### 6. Icon-only buttons are unlabeled
65 icon-only ghost buttons; 24 have an `aria-label` or `title`. The other 41 read as "button" to a screen reader and show no tooltip on hover, so a row of ×, ↻, ✎, ⋯ gives sighted users nothing either. 15 clickable `div`/row elements have no `role` or `tabIndex`, so keyboard users can't reach them.

**Fix:** mechanical, ~2 hours. Add `aria-label` and a `title` to each; add `role="button" tabIndex={0}` plus Enter/Space to the 15 rows.

### 7. Page headers are inconsistent on the deep pages
14 non-public pages don't use `PageHeader`, and they are the ones people spend the most time in: `/campaigns/[id]`, `/clients/[id]`, `/crm/telegram`, `/admin/telegram-comm`, `/reminders`, `/chat`, and the five `/tasks/*` sub-pages. Title weight, icon position and the action slot all differ from the list pages that lead to them, so every drill-down feels like a different app.

**Fix:** ~2 hours. The hero pages (`/campaigns/[id]`, `/clients/[id]`) may deserve a documented exception; the rest are straight swaps.

### 8. Dialog sizing has 10+ ad-hoc widths
`max-w-md` 76, `max-w-[420px]` 29, `max-w-lg` 21, `max-w-sm` 14, `max-w-2xl` 14, `max-w-3xl` 11, `[600px]` 6, `[800px]` 4, `[425px]` 4, `[720px]` 3. Three of those are within 5px of each other. Similar story for primary buttons: 184 default-size brand buttons against 82 `size="sm"`, with no rule for which goes where.

**Fix:** four named dialog sizes (`sm | md | lg | xl`) as a `size` prop on `DialogContent`; a rule that page-header CTAs are `sm` and in-dialog submits are default. Half a day, mostly search-and-replace.

### 9. Five routes render outside the shell
`/settings`, `/mcp`, `/documents/[id]`, `/test-ai`, `/oauth/authorize` have no `layout.tsx`. `/settings` gates auth itself and is a deliberate centered shell, but it is linked from the Reminders setup text and lands the user on a page with **no sidebar and no way back** except the browser button. `/mcp` has no auth gate at all in the page (*verify* that middleware covers it). `/oauth/authorize` is intentional.

**Fix:** give `/settings` a back affordance or the standard layout; confirm `/mcp` is gated; delete `/test-ai`.

### 10. Loading states regress inside dialogs
Page-level loading is good (skeletons that match the loaded layout). Inside dialogs it's back to literal "Loading..." text in 10 components (`TaskDetailModal`, `KrSignalSettingsDialog`, `ActivationSettingsDialog`, `ShowcaseSettingsDialog`, `AddActivationDialog`, `MeetingActionItems`, `FloatingChat`, `DeliverableTemplatesTab`, two public pages).

**Fix:** ~1 hour, swap for `Skeleton` rows.

## Workflow UX (found this week, not from the code sweep)

These matter more than any item above because they cost the team hours and the client real deliverables.

- **KR Signal approvals have been ignored for 8 weeks.** 16 weekly reports sit in `pending_review`. The approve/skip surface is a Telegram card in an ops thread, with no in-app queue, no badge, no nag. A gate nobody sees is a gate that fails closed forever. Needs an in-app "Awaiting your approval" list on `/dashboard` or `/attention` with a one-click approve, and a Monday reminder if Saturday's card is untouched.
- **Ops zero out auto-created payments by hand, daily.** 131 of 134 recent $0 payments were inserted with a real amount and then set to 0 by Quazo or Jdot, because the Telegram approval path creates a per-post payment for KOLs who are paid once per cycle. That is a manual cleanup task we built for them. Needs a "paid per cycle" marker on the campaign KOL so the auto-insert skips, or a single payment per KOL per cycle.
- **Fogo nagged the digest for 16 days** after its term ended because "let it lapse" has no button. Add "Close out engagement" to the client card's menu that flips `is_active` and deactivates recurring deliverables in one step.

## What's already good
- Convention linter: 0 violations across 826 files. The May audit held.
- Native `confirm/alert/prompt`: gone. One shared confirm hook, one native `<select>` left (`KoreaSignalsPanel`).
- `Input` carries the brand focus ring by default, so the 373 inputs without an explicit `focus-brand` class are still correct.
- Paginated where it matters: KOLs (455 rows), Lists, Campaigns, Outreach (1,743 rows, server-side range). Delivery Logs at 458 rows is the one to watch.
- ⌘K search, mobile sidebar drawer, skeletons that match loaded layouts on list pages.
- 1 image without alt text in the whole app.
