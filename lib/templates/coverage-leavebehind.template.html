05How to read the coverage

How this market works. Korean power users live almost entirely on Telegram. Roughly half of what circulates starts there; the other half is imported from global Twitter and adapted. When a major topic lands, the channels that matter tend to cover it inside the same window, so a story placed well moves through the market fast.
How to read Korean Telegram numbers. 1,500 to 3,000 views on a post is generally enough to say it reached a meaningful part of the audience. 5,000 to 10,000 means nearly every active user has seen it at least once.
